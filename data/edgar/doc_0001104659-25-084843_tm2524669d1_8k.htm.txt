Item 4.02 Non-Reliance on Previously Issued Financial Statements
or a Related Audit Report or Completed Interim Review.
&#160;
In
connection with the preparation of VSee Health,&#160;Inc.'s (the "Company")
Annual Report on Form&#160;10-K for the fiscal year ended December&#160;31, 2024 (the "2024 Form&#160;10-K"), the Company
concluded that certain errors (described further below) were made in the consolidated financial statements of VSee Lab,&#160;Inc. ("VSee
Lab"), as of December&#160;31, 2023 and for the year ended December&#160;31, 2023 (on a standalone basis, prior to the closing of
the Company's Business Combination) (collectively, the "Prior Annual Financial Statements"). In addition, these errors
were included in VSee Lab's consolidated financial statements for the periods ended March&#160;31, 2023, June&#160;30, 2023, September&#160;30,
2023, and March&#160;31, 2024 (on a standalone basis, prior to the closing of the Company's Business Combination), as well as the
Company's consolidated financial statements included in the Quarterly Reports on Form&#160;10-Q for the quarterly periods ended
June&#160;30, 2024 and September&#160;30, 2024 (collectively, the "Prior Interim Financial Statements," and together with
the Prior Annual Financial Statements, the "Prior Financial Statements"). On July&#160;17, 2025, management of the Company,
in consultation with the Company's Board of Directors (including the members of the Audit Committee thereof), concluded that the
Prior Financial Statements (other than the March&#160;31, 2023 and March&#160;31, 2024 consolidated financial statements of VSee Lab)
should no longer be relied upon due to the impact of the errors noted below and will be restated. On August&#160;22, 2025, management
of the Company concluded that the March&#160;31, 2023 and March&#160;31, 2024 consolidated financial statements of VSee Lab should no
longer be relied upon due to the impact of the errors noted below and will be restated.
&#160;
The
Prior Financial Statements are required to be restated and should no longer be relied upon due to the following errors: (i)&#160;the failure
to identify and accrue for sales and use taxes in relation to VSee Lab's revenue-generating transactions to taxable customers; (ii)&#160;the
incorrect cutoff of a revenue transaction with a customer as of each of March&#160;31, June&#160;30, September&#160;30, and December&#160;31,
2023; (iii)&#160;the incorrect recognition of accrued expenses of Digital Health Acquisition Corp. as of June&#160;24, 2024, the date
of the Company's initial business combination (the "Business Combination"); (iv)&#160;the incorrect recognition of accrued
expenses of iDoc Virtual Telehealth Solutions,&#160;Inc. ("iDoc") as of the Business Combination date (June&#160;24, 2024);
(v)&#160;the incorrect recognition of certain compensation-related obligations of iDoc as of the Business Combination date; (vi)&#160;the
incorrect recognition of a commitment fee incurred in relation to the equity line of credit purchase agreement as a deferred expense and
an accrued liability as of June&#160;30, 2024; (vii)&#160;the incorrect classification of the acquisition of the remaining non-controlling
interest of This American Doc,&#160;Inc., a wholly-owned subsidiary of VSee Lab, within equity as of the Business Combination date; (viii)&#160;the
incorrect recognition of accrued interest related to certain convertible note obligations that were recorded at fair value in the Company's
financial statements; (ix)&#160;the incorrect recognition of grant date fair value for certain options issued as of June&#160;24, 2024;
(x)&#160;the incurred recognition and measurement of accounts receivable balances acquired from iDoc as of the Business Combination Date;
(xi)&#160;the incorrect recognition of certain income tax related balances as of the Business Combination date; and (xii)&#160;the incorrect
recognition and measurement of income tax-related balances as of and for the periods ended June&#160;30, 2024 and September&#160;30, 2024.
&#160;
As such,
the Company will restate the Prior Financial Statements in the 2024 Form&#160;10-K. The aggregate effects of such errors will be further
described in the 2024 Form&#160;10-K. The Company's management has concluded that, in light of the misstatements described above,
material weaknesses exist in the Company's internal control over financial reporting and that the Company's disclosure controls
and procedures were not effective, which such material weaknesses will be described in more detail in the 2024 Form&#160;10-K.
&#160;
The
management has discussed with WithumSmith+Brown, PC, the Company's independent registered public accounting firm, the matters disclosed
in this Current Report on Form&#160;8-K pursuant to this Item 4.02.
